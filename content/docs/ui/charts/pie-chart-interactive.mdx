Uses `PieChart` with one `Pie` (`innerRadius="64%"`, `outerRadius="88%"`, `startAngle={90}`, `endAngle={-270}`, `cornerRadius={4}`, `isAnimationActive={false}`) and a `shape` function that grows a hovered slice's `Sector` outward by up to `LIFT` pixels. `STORAGE` defines used space per `Kind` for `phone` and `cloud`; `sizes()` turns it into one value per entry in `KINDS`, with `free` as the remainder. `chartConfig` assigns a token to each kind, for example `apps` (`var(--red)`), `photos` (`var(--orange)`) and `free` (`var(--control)`). `React.useState` holds the device and active kind; a `useMorph` hook animates slice sizes on device change and the slice lift on hover. A segmented bar above, the center readout and a legend list all share the active state.

Replace `STORAGE` with your own devices, totals and used amounts, and extend `Kind`, `KINDS` and `chartConfig` to add categories. Keep `free` last in `KINDS` if you want it to fill the remainder of the ring. Adjust `MORPH_DURATION`, `LIFT_DURATION` and `LIFT` to change the animation timing and how far slices lift.

## Notes

`useMorph` jumps straight to the target when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` matches. The center readout is an `aria-live="polite"` region, the segmented bar is `aria-hidden`, and kinds with no data are collapsed and marked `inert` in the list. There is no `ChartTooltip`; the center readout shows the hovered value instead. The `ToggleGroup` has `aria-label="Device"`.
