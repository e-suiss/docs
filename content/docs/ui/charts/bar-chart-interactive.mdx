Uses `BarChart` with three `Bar` elements generated from `CATEGORIES`, all sharing `stackId="a"`; only the top one gets a rounded `radius`. `chartConfig` defines `social` (`var(--blue)`), `productivity` (`var(--cyan)`) and `entertainment` (`var(--orange)`). In week view the data is seven `{ social, productivity, entertainment, label, index }` records built from `USAGE`, with a dashed `ReferenceLine` at the weekly `AVERAGE` and per-bar `Cell` opacity that dims unselected days. In day view `hourly()` derives 24 hourly records for the selected day. A `ToggleGroup` switches `view` with `React.useState`, and the axes swap their `ticks` and `tickFormatter` per view. Below the chart, a summary grid and a "Most used" list update from the selected day or the daily average.

Replace `USAGE` with your own per-day minutes per category, and `PROFILE` and `hourly()` with real hourly data if you have it. To add a category, extend the `Category` type, `CATEGORIES` and `chartConfig`; the bars, tooltip rows and summary grid are all generated from those. Update `APPS` with your app names, categories and `share` fractions.

## Notes

Bars respond to clicks and to taps that move less than `TAP_SLOP` pixels, so scrolling over the chart on touch screens doesn't select a day. A `KeyboardPoint` child tracks the arrow-key position via `useActiveTooltipDataPoints`, and Return or Space selects it. The header is an `aria-live="polite"` region, and the `ToggleGroup` has `aria-label="Period"`. The tooltip `formatter` draws its own color swatch from `var(--color-<name>)`.
