Uses `LineChart` with two `Line` series, `actual` (solid) and `forecast` (`strokeDasharray="5 5"`), both `type="monotone"` and `dot={false}`. `chartData` has twelve `{ month, actual, forecast }` records where `actual` is `null` after September and `forecast` is `null` before it, so the two lines meet at September. A `ReferenceArea` from `Sep` to `Dec` shades the forecast period with `var(--control)`. `chartConfig` gives both series `var(--blue)`.

Fill the `actual` and `forecast` arrays with your values, and keep the overlap point so the dashed line starts where the solid one ends; update the `index >= 8` offset if your split month changes. Set `x1` and `x2` on `ReferenceArea` to the category labels that bound your forecast. Give `forecast` a different color in `chartConfig` if you want stronger contrast.

## Notes

The tooltip uses `indicator="line"`. Because both series share a color, the dash pattern is what distinguishes them. `accessibilityLayer` is set on `LineChart`, so the plot is focusable and arrow keys move the tooltip between points.
