At `md:` the section is a two-column grid with the settings on the left and an 18.75rem preview column on the right; on smaller screens the preview stacks below. Settings are grouped with `ItemGroup`, `Item`, `ItemContent`, `ItemTitle`, `ItemActions`, and `ItemSeparator`: an Allow Notifications `Switch`, three `Toggle` alert styles that each show a small screen illustration, a Banner Style `ToggleGroup`, Sounds and Badges switches, and `Select` menus for previews and grouping. The preview is a phone frame with a wallpaper, clock, and sample notifications that appear, stack, or hide according to the settings.

Alert options come from `alerts`, and the select options from `previewOptions` and `groupingOptions`. Every setting is held in its own `React.useState`. Changing a setting that affects banners calls `replay` to show the sample banner again; with the Temporary style, a `React.useEffect` hides it after 2600 ms. Choosing Never for previews replaces the message text with Notification. Persist each value to your settings store, and replace the `wallpaper` constant and the sample sender and message.

## Notes

Switches are labeled through `label` elements with `htmlFor`, the banner style group has `aria-label="Banner style"`, and each `SelectTrigger` is labeled with its row name. When notifications are off, the dependent settings are `inert` and dimmed. The preview has `role="img"` with an `aria-label` that summarizes the banner behavior, and the alert illustrations are `aria-hidden`. The wallpaper loads from Unsplash; replace it with your own. Notification entry animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
