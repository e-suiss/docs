The block uses the `AppShell` pattern with Summary, Sharing, and category entries in the sidebar (a tab bar on small screens). The page shows a title with `AppShellTrigger` and an `Avatar`, then a list of metric cards. Each card is a link with a tinted category label, a large value with its unit, and a local `Spark` chart that draws either bars or an SVG polyline. On Summary, an Edit `Button` reveals pin and unpin `Button` controls beside each card; on a category page, the cards for that category are listed under Last 7 days.

Edit `categories` for the category names, icons, and colors, and `metrics` for the cards; each metric has a `category`, `label`, `value`, optional `unit`, a `color`, seven `data` points, and an optional `bars` flag. The selected section, the editing state, and the `pinned` labels are held in `React.useState`, with Move, Steps, Resting Heart Rate, and Sleep pinned initially. Card links point to anchors derived from each label, and the time 9:41 and the JR avatar are hard-coded; replace them with your own data and routes.

## Notes

Pin buttons have `aria-label` values such as Pin Sleep or Unpin Move, and an `after:` pseudo-element enlarges their hit area. Sparklines are `aria-hidden` because the value is already shown as text. Caret icons flip with `rtl:rotate-180`, and card entry animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`. When nothing is pinned, the list shows a short message pointing to Edit.
