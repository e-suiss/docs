The section forces the dark palette with the `dark` class. Under a centered heading, a `RadioGroup` lays out three plan cards in one column, or three columns at `md:`. Each card is a `Label` with a visually hidden `RadioGroupItem`, the plan name, audience, monthly price, and a list of services where excluded services are struck through and dimmed. The selected card gains an accent outline. A `Button` and a savings link that updates with the selected plan close the section.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { PriceBundle } from \"@/components/blocks/price-bundle\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<PriceBundle />"} />

Edit `services` (name, icon, and tint classes) and `plans` at the top of the file. Each plan has a `name`, `price`, `people`, `storage`, `saving`, and an `included` count: the first `included` services in the `services` list are shown as included, and the fourth service displays the plan's `storage`. The selected plan is held in `React.useState` and defaults to Family. Give the Try it free button a handler and point the `#compare` link at your comparison page.

## Notes

The group has `aria-label="Plan"`, each radio is labeled with its plan name, and excluded services include visually hidden (not included) text so the strike-through is not the only cue.
