While offline, the section centers an `Empty` composition (`EmptyHeader`, `EmptyMedia`, `EmptyTitle`, `EmptyDescription`, `EmptyContent`) with a Wi-Fi icon, an explanation, and two `Button` actions. Try Again shows a `Spinner` and a Connecting label while it loads. Once connected, the block shows a suiss Music heading and an album grid with two columns, or four from `md:` up, plus a Disconnect link that returns to the offline state.

Replace the `albums` array (`title` and `image`) with your content. The connection state is a `React.useState` value of `offline`, `loading`, or `online`; a `React.useEffect` moves from `loading` to `online` after 1400 ms to simulate a retry. In a real app, drive this state from your network status or a refetch, and give the Downloads button a handler.

## Notes

The Try Again button is disabled while connecting and uses `aria-live="polite"` so the label change is announced. Album artwork is decorative (`alt=""`) and loads from Unsplash through the `photo` helper; replace it with your own. The online view's entry animation is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
