The top of the section is built from `Empty`, `EmptyHeader`, `EmptyMedia`, `EmptyTitle`, `EmptyDescription`, and `EmptyContent`, with a bag icon, a message, and two `Button` actions. Below it, a You may also like list shows recommended products in one column, or three columns from `sm:` up. Each product has an icon `Button` that adds it to or removes it from the bag; once something is added, the bag icon shows a count badge and the title, description, and primary button change to a check out state.

Replace the `recommended` array at the top of the file; each item has a `name`, a preformatted `price` string, and an `image`. The names of added items are held in `React.useState`. The Sign In, Check Out, and Continue Shopping buttons have no handlers, so connect them to your authentication, checkout, and catalog routes, and replace the local add toggle with your cart logic.

## Notes

The title uses `aria-live="polite"` so the item count is announced. Each add button sets `aria-pressed` and an `aria-label` such as Add Pods Studio to bag or Remove Pods Studio from bag, and an `after:` pseudo-element enlarges its hit area. Product images are decorative (`alt=""`) and load from Unsplash through the `photo` helper; replace them with your own.
