From `sm:` the conversation is framed as a phone with a rounded black border and a notch; on the smallest screens it fills the width without the frame. At `md:` a short caption sits beside the phone instead of below it. The thread uses a contact header link with `Avatar` and `AvatarFallback`, a `MessageScroller` of `Message` and `Bubble` items, `BubbleReactions` for hearts, and a composer built from `Input` and a send `Button`.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { ChatThread } from \"@/components/blocks/chat-thread\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<ChatThread />"} />

Replace the `opening` array with the initial messages (`id`, `from`, `text`, and optional `liked`), and the `replies` array with the simulated responses. Messages, the typing indicator, and the draft are held in `React.useState`. Double-clicking a bubble, or pressing Enter on it, toggles a heart through `toggleLike`. The `send` function shows the typing indicator after 500 ms and appends a reply after 1800 ms; replace it with your messaging backend. Update the contact name and initials in the header and point its `#contact` link at a real destination.

## Notes

Each bubble renders as a `button` through `render`, with an `aria-label` that includes the message, whether it is liked, and how to toggle the heart. The viewport is labeled Conversation with Riley, the typing bubble has `aria-label="Typing"`, and the attachment and send buttons are labeled. The notch is `aria-hidden`, and the caret icon flips with `rtl:rotate-180`. Message entry animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
