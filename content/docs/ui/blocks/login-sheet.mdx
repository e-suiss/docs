From `sm:` the screen is framed as a phone with a notch; on the smallest screens it fills the section. The welcome view shows an app icon, title, tagline, and two full-width `Button` actions. Sign in with suiss slides up a custom bottom sheet over a scrim, with the account name, a `RadioGroup` of `RadioGroupItem` and `Label` rows to share or hide the email, and a continue `Button` that shows a `Spinner` while working. After it completes, the welcome view greets the user and offers a Sign out link.

The sheet's open state, the email choice (default `hide`), the busy flag, and the completed state are held in `React.useState`. The `proceed` function simulates a 1000 ms request; replace it with your identity provider's sign-in and send the selected email option. Replace the Trail Routes app name, the tagline, the account name and email, and give the Continue with email button a handler.

## Notes

The sheet has `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, and `aria-labelledby` pointing at its heading, and it is `inert` while closed; the content behind it is `inert` while it is open. Opening moves focus to the Close button, Escape or a scrim click closes the sheet, and focus returns to the trigger. The scrim and notch are `aria-hidden`, and the email `RadioGroup` has `aria-label="Email"`. The sheet's slide transition is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
