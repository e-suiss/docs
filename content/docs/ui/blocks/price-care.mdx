At `md:` the section is a two-column grid: a photo panel with a gradient overlay and headline on the left, and the options on the right; on smaller screens the photo stacks above. A `ToggleGroup` of `ToggleGroupItem` chips selects the device and cross-fades the photo. A `RadioGroup` of `Label` cards with `RadioGroupItem` lists that device's payment options. For Phone Pro, a `Switch` adds theft and loss protection. A footer shows the total and an Add to Bag `Button`.

Edit the `devices` array at the top of the file; each device has a `name`, `image`, `alt`, and `options` with a `label`, `price`, and optional `theft` surcharge. The device, the payment option, and the theft toggle are held in `React.useState`; switching devices resets the option to the device's first. The theft switch is shown only when the device is Phone Pro, so change that check if other devices offer it. Give the Add to Bag button a handler.

## Notes

The device group has `aria-label="Device"`, the options group has `aria-label="Payment"`, and the total uses `aria-live="polite"`. Inactive photos are `aria-hidden`, and each photo has descriptive `alt` text. Images load from Unsplash through the `photo` helper; replace them with your own. The photo cross-fade is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
