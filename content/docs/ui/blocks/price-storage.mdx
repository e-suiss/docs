A centered header with a cloud icon, a heading, and a `ToggleGroup` billing period switch sits above a `RadioGroup` of plan cards that shows one column, two at `sm:`, and four at `lg:`. Each card is a `Label` containing a `RadioGroupItem`, the plan size, the price, and a feature list with check icons; the popular plan carries a Most popular badge, and the selected card lifts with an accent outline. A `Button` at the bottom names the selected plan.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { PriceStorage } from \"@/components/blocks/price-storage\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<PriceStorage />"} />

Edit the `plans` array at the top of the file; each plan has a `size`, a monthly `price`, a list of `features`, and an optional `popular` flag. Yearly prices are computed as ten times the monthly price, matching the 2 months free label. The period, the selected plan, and an `updated` flag are held in `React.useState`. Replace the button's `onClick`, which only sets `updated`, with your billing call.

## Notes

The period group has `aria-label="Billing period"`, the plan group has `aria-label="Storage plan"`, and each radio is labeled with its plan size. The submit button uses `aria-live="polite"` so the confirmation text is announced. The card lift transition is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
