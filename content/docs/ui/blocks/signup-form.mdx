A centered form groups its fields in `fieldset` elements with visible legends: first and last name side by side, a `NativeSelect` country picker stacked with a birthday `Input`, an email `Input` with a hint, and a password and confirmation pair. Fields share bordered stacks that turn red when the passwords do not match. Once a password is typed, a local `PasswordRules` checklist expands below it. A `Button` submits the form, and a success view confirms the new account with a Start over link.

All field values live in a single `form` object in `React.useState`, updated through the `update` helper; `tried` and `done` are also state. The form is valid when the names and birthday are filled, the email matches `EMAIL`, the password reaches a `strength` of 3 (the three `rules` plus a bonus point for a symbol), and both passwords match. Edit `rules` to change the checklist, the country list inline, and replace the success branch with a call to your registration API. The birthday field is free text with `inputMode="numeric"` and is not parsed.

## Notes

Every input has an `aria-label` and an `autoComplete` value (`given-name`, `family-name`, `bday`, `email`, `new-password`). The email references its hint through `aria-describedby`, the confirmation field sets `aria-invalid` on a mismatch, and each rule includes visually hidden (met) or (not met) text. The form error line uses `aria-live="polite"`, and the form uses `noValidate` so the custom validation runs. Transitions are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
