A Store heading and tagline sit beside a short list of shopping help links, wrapping onto separate lines when space runs out. Below, a `Carousel` with `dragFree` scrolling holds one `CarouselItem` per category; each category is a `Toggle` containing an image and a label, and the pressed tile lifts slightly with an accent ring. `CarouselPrevious` and `CarouselNext` sit at the end of the row, and side padding grows at `md:`. A closing line names the selected category.

Replace the `categories` array (`label` and `image`) at the top of the file. The selected category is held in `React.useState` and starts as Phone. Tapping a tile only selects it; navigate or load products for that category from the `Toggle`'s `onPressedChange`. Point the `#specialist` and `#stores` links at your routes.

## Notes

The carousel has `aria-label="Categories"`, and each `Toggle` exposes its pressed state with `aria-pressed`. Category images are decorative (`alt=""`), so each tile is named by its text label. Images load from Unsplash through the `photo` helper; replace them with your own.
