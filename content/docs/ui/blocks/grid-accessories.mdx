A header pairs the section title with a bag icon that shows a count badge once items are added. Category filters are a `ToggleGroup` of `ToggleGroupItem` chips that wrap on narrow screens. Products appear in a two-column grid, which switches at `md:` to an auto-filling grid with columns of at least 12.5rem. Each card has an image, name, price, and an icon `Button` that expands into an In bag pill when the product is added.

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<GridAccessories />"} />

Edit `filters` for the category chips and `accessories` for the products; each product has a `name`, a `category` that must match one of the filters, a preformatted `price`, and an `image`. The active filter and the names in the bag are held in `React.useState`. Replace the local bag toggle with your cart logic.

## Notes

The bag counter uses `role="status"` with an `aria-label` such as 3 items in bag. Each add button sets `aria-pressed` and an `aria-label` naming the product, the visible In bag text is `aria-hidden`, and an `after:` pseudo-element enlarges the hit area. The filter group has `aria-label="Category"`. Product images are decorative (`alt=""`) and load from Unsplash through the `photo` helper; replace them with your own. Card and button animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
