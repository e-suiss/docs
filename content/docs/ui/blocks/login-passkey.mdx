A centered layout with a key icon, a heading, a short explanation, an account chip with `Avatar` and a Change link, a Continue with passkey `Button`, and a link to sign in with a password. Starting the passkey flow opens a compact `Dialog` (`DialogContent`, `DialogTitle`, `DialogDescription`, `DialogClose`) with a pulsing scan glyph that turns into a green check when verified. After verification the screen is replaced by a welcome message with a Sign out link.

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<LoginPasskey />"} />

The flow is a `React.useState` status of `idle`, `scan`, `ok`, or `done`. The `start` function sets timers that move to `ok` after 1500 ms and to `done` after 2400 ms; replace it with a WebAuthn call such as `navigator.credentials.get` and set the status from its result. Closing the dialog calls `cancel`, which clears the timers. Replace the account email, the JR initials, the welcome name, and the `#change` and `#password` links.

## Notes

The dialog has a title and description through `DialogTitle` and `DialogDescription`, and its default close button is hidden in favor of a Cancel button that becomes invisible once verified. Pending timers are cleared when the component unmounts. The scan pulse stops with `motion-reduce:animate-none`, and the welcome entry animation is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
