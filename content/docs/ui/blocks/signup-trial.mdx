At `md:` the section is a two-column grid: a background-image panel with the service name, a headline, and pricing on the left, and the form on the right; on smaller screens the panel stacks above. The form stacks an email and a password `Input` in one bordered group, shows a four-bar strength meter once a password is typed, and adds a terms `Checkbox` with `Label`, a full-width submit `Button`, and a sign-in link. The submit button stays disabled until the form is valid, and a success view confirms the trial.

## Usage

```tsx
import { SignupTrial } from "@/components/blocks/signup-trial"
```

```tsx
<SignupTrial />
```

The email, password, terms flag, and done flag are held in `React.useState`. The `strength` function scores four checks (length, mixed case, a digit, and a symbol), and the form is valid when the email matches `EMAIL`, the score is at least 3, and the terms are accepted. `levels` and `strengthLabel` set the meter colors and spoken labels. Replace the promotional copy, the `image` constant, and the success branch with your own trial signup call, and point `#sign-in` at your sign-in page.

## Notes

The strength meter uses `role="meter"` with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow`, and an `aria-valuetext` such as Fair. Inputs have `aria-label` values and `autoComplete` values of `email` and `new-password`. The promotional panel is a CSS `backgroundImage` loaded from Unsplash, so it has no text alternative; its copy carries the message. Replace the image with your own. Animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
