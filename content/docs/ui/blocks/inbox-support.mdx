Built on the `SplitView` pattern: `SplitViewSidebar` lists the Open, Pending, Solved, and All views with live counts, `SplitViewList` shows tickets as `SplitViewItem` rows with a priority badge and requester `Avatar`, and `SplitViewDetail` shows the selected ticket. The detail pane has a status `ToggleGroup`, the ticket title, the requester, the customer's message, and a reply form with `Textarea` and two `Button` actions. Below `md:` the split view shows one column at a time, starting with the list.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<InboxSupport />"} />

Replace `initialTickets` with your tickets; each has an `id`, `title`, `device`, `status`, `priority`, `requester`, and an avatar `hue`. The `views` array defines the sidebar entries, and `priorityClass` maps each priority to badge colors. Tickets, the active view, the selected ticket, and the reply draft are held in `React.useState`. Sending a reply clears the draft and sets the ticket to Pending, and Mark as solved sets it to Solved; connect both to your help desk API. The customer message and the Today 8:12 AM timestamp are hard-coded for every ticket.

## Notes

The status group has `aria-label="Status"`, and the reply `Textarea` has `aria-label="Reply"`. The detail title is visually hidden with `sr-only`. The detail pane's entry animation is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
