A centered column with a headline that states the bag total, a primary check out `Button`, and a list of line items. Each item uses a two-column grid on small screens and a four-column grid at `md:` with the image, details, quantity, and price side by side. Quantities use the `Stepper` interaction with `StepperInput`, `StepperGroup`, `StepperDecrement`, `StepperSeparator`, and `StepperIncrement`, limited to 1–10. A totals panel lists subtotal, shipping, and total with two check out buttons. When every item is removed, the headline reads Your bag is empty and a Continue Shopping button restores the sample items.

Replace the `initialItems` array at the top of the file; each item has a `name`, `variant`, `price`, `image`, and `quantity`. The list is held in `React.useState`, and removing an item sets its quantity to 0 so it is filtered out. The `money` helper formats US dollars. Connect the Check Out buttons to your checkout flow, point the Show product details links (currently `#details`) at real product pages, and replace the free delivery copy and FREE shipping line if they do not apply.

## Notes

The headline uses `aria-live="polite"` so total changes are announced. Each quantity input has an `aria-label` such as Phone Pro quantity, and each Remove button has an `aria-label` that names the product. Product images are decorative (`alt=""`) and load from Unsplash; replace them with your own. Entry animations use `starting:` styles and are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
