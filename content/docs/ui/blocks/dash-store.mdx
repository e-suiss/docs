The block is wrapped in the `AppShell` pattern with a header, navigation items (one with a badge), a Stores group, and a footer showing the signed-in admin with `Avatar`. The content has a title with `AppShellTrigger` and a range `ToggleGroup`, a two-by-two `dl` of stats, and a Recharts `BarChart` in `ChartContainer` with `ChartTooltip` that dims the bars of other days when one is hovered. Recent orders are filtered by a second `ToggleGroup` and listed with `ItemGroup`, `Item`, `Avatar`, `ItemContent`, `ItemTitle`, `ItemDescription`, and `ItemActions`; order status appears from `sm:` up.

Navigation comes from `items`, the ranges and their multipliers from `ranges`, the chart series from `week`, and colors from `chartConfig`. Orders live in `orders` (`id`, `name`, `product`, `status`, `total`, and an avatar `hue`), and `filters` lists the status filters. The section, range, filter, and hovered day are held in `React.useState`. The stats are computed inside the component by multiplying fixed figures by the range `scale`; replace them, and the hard-coded change percentages, with values from your backend.

## Notes

Both toggle groups are labeled (Range and Order status), the chart headline uses `aria-live="polite"`, and the chart enables Recharts' `accessibilityLayer`. Stats are marked up as a description list with `dt` and `dd`. Order rows use `role="listitem"` inside the list role of `ItemGroup`, and their entry animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
