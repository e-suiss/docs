The block is wrapped in the `AppShell` pattern with an admin navigation and a `Toaster` for confirmations. An inset form built from `ItemGroup`, `Item`, `ItemContent`, `ItemTitle`, `ItemActions`, and `ItemSeparator` holds an email field that turns entries into removable chips, a role `Select`, and a Send welcome email `Switch`. A summary line and a Send Invites `Button` follow. Pending invitations are listed below with `ItemMedia` icons and Resend and Cancel `Button` actions.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { UsersInvite } from \"@/components/blocks/users-invite\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<UsersInvite />"} />

Edit `roles` for the role options and the initial `pending` state for existing invitations. The email chips, the draft text, the role, the welcome switch, and the pending list are held in `React.useState`. An address is committed when you press Enter, type a comma or space, or leave the field, and only addresses that match `EMAIL` and are not already listed are added; Backspace in an empty field removes the last chip. Sending moves the chips into the pending list and shows a toast through the module-level `toasts` manager from `createToastManager`. Replace these handlers with calls to your invitation API.

## Notes

The email input is a native `input` labeled through `label` and `htmlFor`, and each chip has a remove button with an `aria-label` such as Remove avery@company.com. The role `SelectTrigger` has `aria-label="Role"`, the welcome `Switch` is labeled through `htmlFor`, and the summary line uses `aria-live="polite"`. Pending rows animate in, and the animation is disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
