Built on the `SplitView` pattern. `SplitViewList` holds an Inbox title, a `ToggleGroup` of category chips, and the messages as `SplitViewItem` rows; `SplitViewDetail` shows the selected message in a local `Reader` component. Each category chip shows only its icon until selected, when it expands to show its label in a category tint, and unselected chips show a dot when they contain unread mail. The reader has a toolbar of ghost icon `Button` actions (Reply, Star, Archive, Delete), a sender `Avatar`, the message, and a reply form with `Textarea`. Below `md:` the split view shows one column at a time, starting with the list.

Replace `initialMail` with your messages; each has an `id`, `from`, `subject`, `body`, `time`, an avatar `hue` (0 renders a monogram), a `category`, and `unread` and `starred` flags. The `categories` array sets each chip's icon and tint. The mail, active category, and selected message are held in `React.useState`, and the `Reader` keeps its own reply state. Opening a message marks it read; Star toggles `starred`; Archive and Delete both remove the message from the list. The reply form only clears and closes on submit, so connect it and the toolbar actions to your mail API. The recipient line To: Jamie Rivera is hard-coded.

## Notes

The category group has `aria-label="Category"`, and each chip has an `aria-label` with its name. Unread dots include visually hidden Unread text, and the star icon in a row has `aria-label="Starred"`. Toolbar buttons are labeled, and the Star button exposes `aria-pressed`. The reply `Textarea` has an `aria-label` such as Reply to Morgan Lee and receives focus when it opens. The detail title is visually hidden with `sr-only`. Entry animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
