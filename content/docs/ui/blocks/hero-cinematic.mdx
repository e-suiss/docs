The section forces the dark palette with the `dark` class and sets a `--finish` CSS variable from the selected finish. That variable tints a radial glow behind the content and the gradient on the large title, which grows at `md:`. A wide `2:1` image area stacks one image per finish and cross-fades to the active one. Below it, the finish name, a `ToggleGroup` of round `ToggleGroupItem` swatches, a `Button`, and a Watch the film link complete the hero.

Edit the `finishes` array at the top of the file; each finish has an `id`, `label`, a CSS `color` (the sample uses theme variables such as `var(--orange)`), an `image`, and `alt`. The selected finish `id` is held in `React.useState`. Change the headline and subheading inline, give the Buy button a handler, and point the `#film` link at your video.

## Notes

The decorative glow is `aria-hidden`, as are the inactive images. The finish name uses `aria-live="polite"` so it is announced when the selection changes. The swatch group has `aria-label="Finish"`, and each swatch is labeled with its finish name. Images load from Unsplash through the `photo` helper; replace them with your own. Entry and cross-fade animations are disabled with `motion-reduce:`.
