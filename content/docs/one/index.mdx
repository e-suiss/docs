## What One is

Esuiss One is a user-owned, model-independent personal agent that persists across devices and contexts. It learns how its user lives and works, coordinates models, tools and other agents behind one identity, and progressively acts on the user's behalf through the smallest appropriate interface.

It is not designed as a chatbot, a single LLM, a super-app or the assistant of one particular device. Its core is a lasting relationship between a person and their own agent, one that continues across devices and model providers.

## The thesis

> You → One → Everything

To the outside, One behaves as a single identity. On the inside, it coordinates different reasoning providers, specialist agents, tools and capabilities from Esuiss and third parties.

## Product principles

- Chat is a modality, not the product.
- One is the relationship; models are reasoning providers.
- The agent belongs to the person, not to the device.
- Tasks belong to One, not to a conversation or endpoint.
- Full UI is optional; presence is fundamental.
- One uses the smallest sufficient surface.
- Multi-agent internally, singular identity externally.
- Observation, inference, memory and authority are separate concepts.
- Prediction does not create authority.
- Structured, native capabilities beat pixel automation whenever possible.
- User attention is scarce; One minimizes unnecessary interaction rather than maximizing engagement.
- One acts for the user, not for advertisers.
- Memory, identity, task state and authority remain independent of the current model provider.
- Protocols are adapters; canonical One semantics belong to One.
- One can use Esuiss products deeply without depending on them exclusively.
