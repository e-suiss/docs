## What Work is

Work is a coordination system. When people, AI agents, systems and machines work on the same thing, Work derives from the work's own records:

- who has the work, and who promised what to whom;
- what is awaited, what is blocked, from whom and by when;
- which decision or approval is pending, and who will give it;
- who is acting on whose behalf, and who is accountable;
- whose attention is needed right now, and what happens if they wait.

It records commitments, not just tasks, and keeps a signed history of who said what, when and on whose behalf. Work answers a single question:

> In this work, who promised what to whom, what is awaited, and whose attention is needed right now?

## Why Work

Task boards track cards in columns. Real work is made of promises: someone asks, someone commits, someone delivers and someone accepts. When a promise slips, the people who depend on it should know before the deadline, not after.

AI agents make this harder. An agent can take work, run for hours on another machine, get stuck on a question or need a human to approve a step. Work gives people and agents one shared record of commitments, waits and decisions, and puts the few things that need a person in front of that person.

Work is not designed around any profession. Domain vocabulary is added on top as Practices.

## Features

| Area | What the specification covers |
| --- | --- |
| Commitments | Request, promise, deliver and accept, with counter-offers, conditional promises and four-eyes acceptance; delegation that keeps the original promise visible; budgets, SLAs and amendments; every change a typed, signed record |
| Attention | Needs Attention, showing only items that need you and why now; Briefing, showing what changed while you were away; addressed waits; escalation delivered through Relay |
| People and agents | Work handed to agent fleets across machines; an accountable owner and a single driver; gates for decisions and acceptance; agent output treated as evidence, never authority |
| Trust and history | An append-only, signed ledger with checkpoints and independent witnesses; offline verification of any record; cross-organization work where the origin owns its records; no physical deletes |
| Open and extensible | Practices authored in a visual editor, as YAML or with an SDK; REST API, CloudEvents, signed webhooks, A2A and MCP; import from Jira, Linear or CSV |

## What Work is not

- **Not an execution engine.** Work records and derives; it does not execute.
- **Not an identity or authority system.** [Access](/docs/access) owns identity and authority. Work records which authority was used.
- **Not a notification system.** [Relay](/docs/relay) delivers notifications, real-time streams, waits and escalations.
- **Not a task manager or a workflow engine.** The unit of work is a commitment, and state is never moved by hand.
- **Not an agent marketplace.** Work does not pick or rank "the best agent".

## Firm rules

- **"Done" is not acceptance.** Work ends with the acceptance of the obligee or a verifier named in advance.
- **Silence is not approval.** An unanswered decision is never a "yes".
- **An agent is never accountable on its own.** The chain of accountability ends in a person or an organization.
- **Work never creates authority.** It can only narrow it.
- **No physical deletion.** Erasing personal data makes the content inaccessible; the record skeleton stays.
