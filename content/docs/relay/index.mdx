## What Relay is

Relay is a notification, messaging and event orchestration platform. When a product or system says "this happened", Relay takes that event and:

- decides who is notified, through which channel and when;
- applies consent and regulatory rules;
- prepares the message in the right language and in a form suited to the channel;
- hands it to the provider, retrying or falling over to another channel when needed;
- records what happened, with evidence: delivered, failed, unknown, or **why it was not sent**.

Relay delivers to people, services, devices and AI agents with the same engine. Relay answers a single question:

> How will this event or message reach the right person, service, device or agent?

## Why Relay

Every product ends up rebuilding the same notification plumbing: channel selection, retries, provider failover, preference centers, quiet hours, consent registries, delivery tracking, in-app inboxes and webhooks. When each product writes these separately:

| Problem | Outcome | Relay's answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rules are scattered | One product applies quiet hours, another does not | A single decision layer whose gates cannot be bypassed with a flag |
| "Did it go out?" is unknown | Nobody can find out why a code didn't arrive | An append-only delivery ledger. Every message not sent is visible with a reason code |
| Agents are left unserved | An agent's question to a human is lost or delayed | Wait points, an early-reply buffer and durable mailboxes |

## Features

| Area | What the specification covers |
| --- | --- |
| Channels | Push (APNs, FCM, Web Push, HMS), email, SMS, voice; WhatsApp, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Telegram; in-app inbox with real-time updates; webhooks and event destinations such as Kafka, NATS, SQS and S3 |
| Orchestration | Workflows from a visual editor, YAML/JSON files or an SDK, all in one format; routing with fallback, fan-out and escalation; digest, throttle, deduplication, scheduling; topics and sub-tenants |
| Reliability | Idempotent API; at-least-once delivery with deduplication; provider error classification and circuit breakers; delivery state from an append-only ledger, with an honest `unknown`; kill switch and priority lanes for OTP traffic |
| Consent and compliance | Message classes that drive the rules; a preference center; Turkey's İYS consent registry; one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058); per-country rules for TR, EU and US; no physical deletes |
| AI agents | Wait points that never lose an early reply; durable mailboxes with lease/ack; escalation chains; A2A, MCP and AG-UI support |

## What Relay is not

- **Not a source of authority or approval.** Delivery, acknowledgement, "seen" and channel replies are never authority. [Access](/docs/access) decides.
- **Not a durable execution engine.** Relay holds the wait point and the delivery. Checkpointing and resuming belong to the waiting side.
- **Not an identity provider.** Identities come from Access.
- **Not a system that generates or verifies OTP codes.** It delivers them.
- **Not a platform that runs customer code.** Customization is done through data, templates and allowlists.
